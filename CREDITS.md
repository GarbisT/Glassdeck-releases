## How to appear here

Being named is opt-in, and it stays opt-in. Sponsoring does not put your name
in this file, because your name is yours and publishing it should be your
decision rather than a side effect of a payment.

To be listed, say so in the message when you sponsor, or
[open an issue](https://github.com/GarbisT/Glassdeck-releases/issues) asking to
be added. Tell me exactly how you want to be written: a full name, a handle, a
first name, a company, whatever you prefer, and a link if you want one.

Any amount qualifies. The higher tiers mention this file because that is where
it is worth advertising, not because five dollars is worth less thanks.

## How to be removed

[Open an issue](https://github.com/GarbisT/Glassdeck-releases/issues) or email,
and it is taken out in the next commit. No reason needed, and no questions
asked. That holds whether or not the sponsorship is still active.

## What it is not

Being in this file is not an endorsement of GlassDeck by anybody named in it,
and it grants no rights over the software. The licence is unchanged by it, and
sponsorship unlocks no features: the app is free, complete, and identical for
everybody.
